# 👥 HR Analytics Dashboard | Microsoft Power BI

## 📖 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end **HR Analytics Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI**. The objective was to analyze employee data, identify the key factors contributing to employee attrition, and generate actionable insights that support data-driven Human Resource decisions.

The dashboard enables HR professionals and business leaders to monitor workforce trends, evaluate employee demographics, and identify high-risk groups to improve employee retention and organizational performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Employee attrition can significantly impact organizational productivity, recruitment costs, and overall business performance. The objective of this project is to identify the primary drivers of employee attrition and provide data-driven recommendations that help improve employee engagement, retention, and workforce planning.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition across different demographic groups.
- Identify key factors influencing employee turnover.
- Monitor workforce metrics using an interactive dashboard.
- Support HR decision-making through meaningful business insights.
- Recommend strategies to improve employee retention and organizational performance.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

- Employees with a **Life Sciences** educational background recorded the highest attrition rate, contributing approximately **38%** of total employee attrition.
- The **26–35 years** age group experienced the highest employee turnover, indicating that mid-career professionals are more likely to leave the organization.
- Employees earning **up to 5K** exhibited the highest attrition rate, suggesting that compensation may be a significant contributing factor.
- Employees with **0–2 years** of experience showed the highest turnover, highlighting challenges in retaining newly hired employees.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following strategies are recommended:

- Implement structured employee recognition and reward programs to improve engagement and motivation.
- Invest in continuous learning, training, and career development opportunities.
- Promote work-life balance initiatives to reduce employee stress and burnout.
- Benchmark compensation packages against industry standards to remain competitive.
- Strengthen leadership development and mentorship programs for early-career employees.
- Conduct comprehensive exit interviews to identify recurring causes of employee turnover.
- Regularly analyze employee feedback and implement corrective actions to improve workplace satisfaction.
